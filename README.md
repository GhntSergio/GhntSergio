### 🌟 About Me:
Atlassian Consultant at **Devoteam**, with 4+ years administering Atlassian Cloud and Data Center for large enterprise accounts (energy, fintech, automotive, healthcare, retail). I work across the full Atlassian scope:

- **Administration & RUN**: Jira Software, Jira Service Management, Confluence, Assets, at site level, plus governance of Rovo AI agents
- **ITSM**: JSM portals, workflows, SLAs, queues, complex automations, dynamic AQL filters on Assets
- **Cloud migration**: Data Center to Cloud assessments, JCMA/CCMA impact analysis, Marketplace apps compatibility (ScriptRunner, Structure, Xray, Advanced Roadmaps)
- **Security & identity**: Atlassian Guard, SSO with Azure AD, SCIM, MFA
- **Reporting**: eazyBI and Tempo dashboards for SLA tracking and ITSM analysis (Change, Service Request, Incident)

Before moving to Atlassian, I worked as a **Data Scientist**. That background shapes how I work today: automation, scripting (Python, REST APIs) and data-driven decisions in every Atlassian project.
